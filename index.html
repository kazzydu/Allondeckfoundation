Tennis should be a pathway, not a privilege.

Across Africa, gifted juniors stall at the step that costs money: coaching, kit, tournament entry. The AllONDECK Foundation closes that gap with grassroots coaching, free tennis education, and grants that put juniors into international competition.

The problem

What it really costs a junior to compete

The ITF World Tennis Tour Juniors is how young players earn rankings, experience and the attention of academies and colleges. Each step costs money: entry fees, travel, accommodation, equipment.

We asked the five families in our 2026 pilot what competing would really cost. Even with our grants, nearly half of it had no source.

2026 pilot, five juniors
Families' estimate of the full cost₦1,686,000
Our grants₦470,000
Families' own contribution₦435,000
Still to find₦781,000

About ₦337,200 per junior. Figures are the families' own estimates.

How we work

Four steps, each free to the player

Most young players drop out at the step that costs money. We pay for the steps families can't.

  1. Discover

    Grassroots clinics bring the game to new courts and find players who have never been coached.

    Grassroots clinics

  2. Learn

    Free online courses in technique, tactics, fitness and the mental game, each ending with a certificate.

    Tennis Education

  3. Compete

    Grants toward ITF junior tournament entry for players who have earned the chance.

    Tournament Access Grants

  4. Report back

    We record what every grant paid for and what the player achieved, and publish it.

    Progress reports

Theory of change. If talented juniors can afford to compete internationally and are supported to learn on and off the court, they gain match experience, rankings and a record that academies, colleges and federations can see.

Accountability

How we handle money and children's privacy

  • Separate recordsFoundation donations and grants are recorded apart from AllONDECK HUB's business income.
  • Clear criteriaEligibility, the event's requirements, financial need and the funding available. How grants work
  • Safeguarding and privacyGuardian consent is required for under-18s. No child's name, photo or family circumstances are published without consent; we publish totals, not names.
  • ReportingThe 2026 pilot progress report is published. An annual report will follow.
