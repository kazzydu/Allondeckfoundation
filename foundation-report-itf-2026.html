ITF Tournament Access Pilot 2026: progress report

Published 15 November 2026 · AllONDECK Foundation

In October 2026 the AllONDECK Foundation ran its first grant round. Junior players applied for help with the cost of competing on the ITF World Tennis Tour Juniors in Nigeria. This report sets out what we committed, what families told us they needed, and what comes next.

What competing really costs

Families estimated the full cost of their tournament plans at ₦1,686,000 across the five players. They planned to put in ₦435,000 themselves. Our grants of ₦470,000 cover about 28% of the total.

Foundation grants ₦470,000 Families ₦435,000 Still to find ₦781,000

The gap is the point. Even with a grant, nearly half the cost of competing internationally had no source. That is the step where talented juniors fall off the pathway.

What families asked for

  • Tournament entry fees: all 5 players
  • Accommodation: 3 players
  • Transport: 2 players
  • Equipment: 2 players

Grants in this round go toward ITF tournament entry.

Why we don't name the players

A family's finances are private, and most of this cohort are children. We publish totals, not names. A player's story is shared only with their permission, or a guardian's for under-18s.

What comes next

We are planning the next round. More funding means more juniors backed, and grants that cover more of the real cost: travel and accommodation as well as entry.

Figures are taken from our grant records on the date above. "Committed" means approved for payment. We will update this report when the round closes.
